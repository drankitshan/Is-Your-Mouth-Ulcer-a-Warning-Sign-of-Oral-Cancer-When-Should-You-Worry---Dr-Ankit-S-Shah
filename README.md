Is Your Mouth Ulcer a Warning Sign of Oral Cancer? When Should You Worry? - Dr Ankit S Shah

Most mouth ulcers heal within 7–14 days and are harmless. However, any ulcer that persists beyond two weeks should never be ignored. Early clinical evaluation can help detect serious conditions like oral cancer at a stage when treatment is more effective and outcomes are significantly better. Don't rely solely on home remedies or painkillers—timely examination can make all the difference.
